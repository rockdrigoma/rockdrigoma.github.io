Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to leave my Mac awake for the schedule to trigger?

Yes. macOS cannot run tasks while asleep. We recommend scheduling during active hours or setting your Mac not to sleep prior to the scheduled send time.

Is my WhatsApp data safe?

100%. All WhatsApp session files are stored locally in your user folder with restricted permissions (POSIX 0700). Nothing is ever transmitted to external servers.
