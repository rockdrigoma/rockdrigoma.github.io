Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

Scheduler for WhatsApp is a desktop client software that operates entirely locally on your macOS device. We do not operate external backend servers to collect, store, or process your WhatsApp messages, chat lists, contacts, or phone numbers.

2. Local Session Storage

Your WhatsApp authentication session is stored solely within your local macOS user profile directory with restricted POSIX 0700 file permissions.

3. Payment & Licensing Processing

Payment processing for Pro licenses is handled securely by Lemon Squeezy. We do not store or process your credit card or financial billing information.

4. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact support@mantiscorp.io.
