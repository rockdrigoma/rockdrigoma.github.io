Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Zero-Data-Collection Principle

MantisCorp builds software centered around user sovereignty and local execution. Scheduler for WhatsApp is a native macOS application that operates 100% on your local machine. We do not maintain any cloud databases, external analytics trackers, or remote servers that collect, store, inspect, or intercept your personal messages, contact lists, conversation histories, or phone numbers.

2. Local Session Storage & Security

All automation data, schedules, and WhatsApp Web browser profiles are stored exclusively within your local macOS user directory (~/.whatsapp_scheduler/). These files are protected using restricted POSIX 0700 / 0600 file system permissions, accessible only by your local macOS account.

3. Payments & Billing Security

Purchases and license key issuance are processed through certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant merchant partners (such as Gumroad, Stripe, or Lemon Squeezy). When purchasing a license:

  • Your credit card numbers, billing addresses, and payment credentials are encrypted and processed directly by the payment processor. MantisCorp never has access to or stores your payment details.
  • We only receive basic order metadata (email address, license key, and order ID) for the sole purpose of customer support and license activation verification.

4. Telemetry & Analytics

Scheduler for WhatsApp does not contain background tracking SDKs, advertising IDs, or behavioral analytics beacons. The software communicates over the internet exclusively when you perform a manual action to activate or verify your software license key.

5. Third-Party Trademarks & Independence

WhatsApp is a registered trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc. Scheduler for WhatsApp is an independent software tool and is not affiliated with, authorized, maintained, or endorsed by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc.

6. Contacting Us

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or data handling, please contact us at privacy@mantiscorp.io.
