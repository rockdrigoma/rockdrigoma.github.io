What happens if my Mac is asleep? Does it support Auto-Wake?

Yes! The app features built-in macOS Hardware Auto-Wake powered by pmset. It programs your Mac's internal RTC hardware clock to automatically power on and wake your machine 2 minutes before your scheduled send time (requires keeping your Mac connected to its power adapter). You can also safely close the app window and let Apple's native launchd daemon handle execution in the background.
